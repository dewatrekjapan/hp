02 ／ Why Shonai

I am not from here.

I moved to Shonai from Chiba, near Tokyo, and started the company here. Coming from outside is what let me see it.

Perfectly usable houses and shops sit empty here at prices a city buyer would not believe. Locally, the same buildings are quietly written off as beyond saving. That gap in perception is the whole opportunity.

We avoid the language of “contributing to the region”, because that does not last. It has to pay, so that it can continue — and only what continues brings a building back. That is the order things happen in.

So we do not build plans that depend on subsidies, and we decline projects where the numbers do not work. Refusing to talk in platitudes is the most honest thing we can do.

Mission

Give what has fallen out of use
a next purpose.

Vision

A Shonai where buildings and work
keep moving, even as the population falls.

  1. Values 01

    Start from facts

    We separate what we have verified, what we infer, and what remains unknown — every time. We do not present only the convenient figures. It is a habit from audit work, and the last thing we would give up.

  2. Values 02

    Hand over something that works

    Not a deck. Letting terms, process flows, running tools — things you can use the next morning. We run the same tools in our own company every day.

  3. Values 03

    Say what we cannot do, first

    We do not take work that requires a licence we do not hold. We do not guarantee outcomes. When a project should be dropped, we say so and explain why. Declining is part of the job.
